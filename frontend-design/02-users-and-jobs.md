# 02. Users and Jobs

> All user profiles below are INFERRED from the return-gift/gifting market category and the reference businesses. None are CONFIRMED for Gifties For You specifically — the client has not yet described their actual customer base.

## Primary User: The Occasion Shopper

### Who They Are
Someone shopping for a specific event — wedding, birthday, housewarming, baby shower, festival/pooja, or a corporate function — who needs multiple identical/similar gift items (return gifts) or a smaller number of curated gifts. INFERRED from market category.

### Technical Knowledge
Comfortable with basic mobile browsing and WhatsApp; not necessarily comfortable with account creation, multi-step checkout forms, or desktop-first e-commerce. INFERRED (mobile-first, India, local-business context — consistent with other Fresh Frame clients).

### Primary Goal
Find enough gift items, at the right price point, for their event, and place an order/enquiry with minimum friction — ideally without creating an account. INFERRED.

### Secondary Goals
- Compare a few product options before committing
- Understand if customization/personalization is possible (name printing, packaging, colors)
- Confirm pricing scales sensibly for bulk quantities
All INFERRED from market pattern (all 4 references foreground price-per-unit and bulk framing).

### Frustrations (inferred from market gaps)
- Having to create an account or use a full cart just to ask "can I get 100 of these by next week?"
- Not knowing if an item can be customized before enquiring
- Not knowing the real price until deep in a checkout flow
- No easy way to browse "what's available under ₹X"

### Frequency of Use
Low-frequency, high-intent — most visits are tied to a specific upcoming event, not casual/recurring browsing (unlike a general e-commerce site). INFERRED.

### Important Actions
Browse by occasion or price → view product detail → enquire (WhatsApp, per the agreed scope) with product + quantity context carried into the message.

### Information They Need
Price (per unit and, ideally, at bulk quantities), product photos, what occasions it suits, whether it can be customized, minimum order quantity if any, delivery/pickup info.

## Secondary User: The Repeat/Bulk Planner

### Who They Are
Someone planning a larger function (a wedding, a corporate event) who may order in bulk across multiple product types, possibly returning to the business more than once. INFERRED from the "Bulk Orders" pattern common across all 4 references and Wedtree's dedicated corporate-gifting subsidiary.

### Primary Goal
Get a fast, accurate answer on bulk pricing and availability without navigating a full storefront checkout.

### Frustrations
Generic contact forms with no product context attached; having to re-explain what they want over multiple messages.

### Primary Action
Enquire directly (WhatsApp) with specific product(s) and quantity pre-filled or easy to state.

### Who They Are
The client — runs the shop, currently manages sales via Instagram. CONFIRMED to be the paying client for this project.

### Primary Goal
A website that generates and organizes enquiries without requiring them to manage a cart/inventory/admin system — CONFIRMED, this is the explicit scope decision already made ("simple site, no cart/admin").

### Important Actions
Receiving enquiries (WhatsApp), keeping the site content (photos, prices) easy enough to hand off updates to Fresh Frame periodically — no self-service admin panel in this scope.
